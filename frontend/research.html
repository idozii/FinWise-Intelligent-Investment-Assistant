02 / METHODOLOGY

Built around reproducibility, not hype.

01

Time-respecting evaluation

Use matured labels at each forecast origin, independent source fingerprints, and explicit historical test boundaries.

02

Strong simple baselines

Compare Ridge to Naive persistence and report negative results instead of claiming automatic AI superiority.

03

Calibrated uncertainty research

Measure marginal interval coverage, width, drift and the cost of applying an abstention threshold.

04

Data integrity auditing

Document splits, event announcement timing and differing raw/adjusted price definitions before interpreting losses.

03 / RELIABILITY

Sometimes the right output is “no forecast”.

A width-based policy can reject uncertain predictions. But abstention has a cost: fewer reported forecasts, small selected samples, and no automatic conditional-coverage guarantee.
